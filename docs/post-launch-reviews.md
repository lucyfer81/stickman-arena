# Stickman Arena — Post-Launch Steam Reviews (Simulated)

Pretend the game shipped. These 40 reviews are written from the perspective of
real Steam players, grounded in the game's actual features (procedural stickman
art, hit-stop/screen-shake juice, Second Wind "broken/reform" mechanic, boss
every 5 waves, shielder/bomber/ranger enemies, daily challenge, SFX-only audio,
keyboard-only J/K controls, mobile touch overlay). Each is tagged with the
complaint cluster it belongs to for the analysis below.

## 20 Positive Reviews

**1. [Recommended] (4.2 hrs on record)**
Genuinely the most *juicy* free game I've played in a browser. The hit-stop on
a clean kick, the camera punch-in, the slow-mo on a kill — every connection
feels like it weighs something. The Second Wind thing caught me totally off
guard the first time: I died, my guy's arm flew off, and I somehow killed two
enemies with one arm and picked up a health pack to snap back. I actually
yelled. *(juice, second wind)*

**2. [Recommended] (1.8 hrs)**
I came in expecting a 5-minute novelty and stayed for the daily challenge. The
modifier rotation keeps it fresh and the separate daily best gives me a reason
to open it every morning with my coffee. Small game, big hooks. *(daily, hooks)*

**3. [Recommended] (9.4 hrs)**
The boss fights are the highlight. Every 5 waves you get this big 1.6x elite
that slams the ground and sends shockwaves you have to jump. The first time I
killed one at 8 HP with the slow-mo and the particle burst I felt like an
absolute god. More of THIS please. *(boss)*

**4. [Recommended] (3.1 hrs)**
For something with zero art assets this looks incredible. The stickmen are drawn
from a skeleton, the background has parallax and drifting embers, the neon lines
pop. It runs on a potato. Respect the craft. *(art, performance)*

**5. [Recommended] (6.7 hrs)**
Combat has real depth once you learn it. Punch is your safe combo starter, kick
is the committed finisher, and if you whiff a kick you eat endlag — so spamming
K actually gets you punished by runners. The punch->kick cancel flows so nice.
*(depth, combat)*

**6. [Recommended] (2.2 hrs)**
The enemy variety carries the mid-game. Shielders force you to kick or flank,
bombers make you space out (and you can bait them into blowing up other
enemies, which is hilarious), rangers make you close distance. Each one demands
a different answer. *(enemy variety)*

**7. [Recommended] (12 hrs)**
Unlocked all 4 skins and chasing the career stats is my current comfort game.
The toxic and royal palettes look slick. It's the kind of thing I boot between
matches of bigger games. *(meta, progression)*

**8. [Recommended] (0.8 hrs)**
Played one run, got the "REFORMED!" pop after a desperate broken-state comeback,
immediately bought two friends a link. That mechanic alone is worth the
click. *(second wind)*

**9. [Recommended] (4.5 hrs)**
Combo system finally clicked. The 2.2s window with the milestone tiers (NICE ->
GODLIKE) and the floating multipliers make chaining feel rewarding instead of

**10. [Recommended] (1.1 hrs)**
The FIRST BLOOD slow-mo on your first kill of a run is such a smart onboarding
touch — it teaches you immediately that hitting things = good and feels
celebratory. The teaching hints in wave 1-2 helped too. *(onboarding)*

**11. [Recommended] (5.3 hrs)**
Difficulty select is appreciated. Easy is genuinely chill, Hard makes the

**12. [Recommended] (2.9 hrs)**

**13. [Recommended] (7.0 hrs)**
Rare events are great. Got a METEOR STORM wave and a BOMB SQUAD wave back to

**14. [Recommended] (3.8 hrs)**

**15. [Recommended] (1.5 hrs)**

**16. [Recommended] (10 hrs)**
I'm a sucker for a good K.O. The kill slow-mo (0.35x) into the particle burst

### Verdict
**The single most common complaint is NO MUSIC** — 6 of 20 negative reviews
(30%), and it's the only complaint that *every* player experiences regardless of
skill or platform. It is also the most severe because it undercuts the game's
strongest asset (its juice): all that hit-stop and slow-mo plays out in dead
air.

### Root cause (verified in source)
`js/systems/AudioManager.js` contains ONLY one-shot procedural SFX
(`punch/kick/hit/jump/...`). There is **no music system whatsoever** — no
`startMusic`, no `bgm`, no loop scheduler, no `musicGain`. A repo-wide search for
`music|bgm|BGM|ambient|startMusic` returns nothing. No scene ever starts a
continuous audio bed, so the title screen, every wave, every boss, and every
Second Wind moment play out in total silence (punctuated only by SFX).

### Fix (this iteration)
Add a **procedural generative music engine** to `AudioManager` — fitting the
game's "100% procedural, no external files" ethos (same approach as the SFX). It
must:
- Run a lookahead scheduler that loops a generative groove (bass + arp + sparse
  percussion) on its own gain node (duckable, respects volume/mute).
- React to intensity: menu (calm) -> combat -> boss (intense) -> broken
  (tense), driven by the scenes.
- Start on the title (after the gesture-resume), follow the player into combat,
  intensify on boss/broken, stop on game over, duck on pause.
- Be observable for tests via `window.__stickman.music`.

This is then iterated: fix -> test -> write a second batch of reviews -> repeat.
